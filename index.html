ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm CrabbyPatrick

better known as Patrick J. Pardillo. I'm currently a student exploring the worlds of programming. This space serves as my digital playground and prototype portfolio, where I document my journey learning software development. Alongside coding, I love experimenting with 2D game design, website design, and photo editing. Since everything here is a work in progress, you'll see a mix of hands-on experiments, learning milestones, and creative prototypes as I continue building my skills step by step!

I view every line of code as an experiment, a theory waiting to be tested and refined. In this stage of my journey, my focus isn't just on building systems that function, but on truly grasping the core logic behind the technology. This prototype site isn't a final showcase of mastery, but a transparent log of my trial, error, and growth. By bridging technical precision with creative expression, I hope to eventually craft digital solutions that deliver genuine value to the community.
